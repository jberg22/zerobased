Who we are

ZeroBased LLC ("ZeroBased," "we," "us") makes the ZeroBased app for iPhone and runs zerobased.org. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, or the identifier Apple provides if you use Sign in with Apple. [Your name, if you add one.]
  • Information from accounts you connect. Account names and types, balances, and transactions, received through Plaid.
  • Information you create. Budgets, categories, rules, notes, and settings such as your theme.
  • Subscription status. Whether your subscription is active, as confirmed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Launch list. If you sign up on zerobased.org to hear when ZeroBased launches, your email address, collected through our website host, Netlify.
  • [Diagnostics and usage data, if you add crash reporting or analytics.]

Bank connections

When you connect an account, you sign in to your bank through Plaid Inc., not through ZeroBased. Plaid then shares read-only account information with us. We never receive your bank username or password. Plaid's own handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

How we use it

To run the app: syncing and categorizing transactions, applying your rules, calculating budgets and pace, finding recurring charges, and building reports. We also use it to answer support requests, to send the launch email you asked for, and to keep the service secure. [We do not use your financial information for advertising.]

AI assistant

The assistant is optional. When you ask it a question, your question and the transaction and budget details needed to answer it are sent to the AI provider you've selected [list providers] to generate a reply. [Describe each provider's retention and training terms.]

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Google Cloud (Firebase) and processed on Amazon Web Services, in the United States [confirm regions]. A copy is kept on your iPhone so the app stays fast. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Who we share with

Only the service providers that help us run ZeroBased (Plaid, Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services, Netlify [and AI providers]), when the law requires it, or when you ask us to. We do not sell personal information.

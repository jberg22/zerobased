Getting started

Do I need an account to try ZeroBased?

No. Choose the demo when you open the app to explore it with sample data. Create an account when you're ready to connect your own banks.

How do I set up my first budget?

After you connect your accounts, the Budget tab walks you through setup. Pick a category budget or fixed & flex, and ZeroBased fills in starting amounts from your recent spending.

What does "Ahead of pace" mean?

Your spending is running faster than the month, for example 60% of your budget spent with 40% of the month gone. You're still under budget; it's a nudge, not an alarm.

Bank connections

Is my bank supported?

ZeroBased connects through Plaid, which supports thousands of US banks, credit unions and card issuers. You can search for yours from the demo before you subscribe.

Why is a transaction missing or still pending?

Banks share transactions on their own schedule. Pending charges can take a few days to post, and the amount can change when they do, for example when a tip is added.

Can I connect more than one bank?

Yes. Add each bank from the Accounts tab. Accounts are grouped by bank, and your budget and reports include all of them.

Subscription & billing

How does the free trial work?

Your trial starts when you subscribe after creating an account. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy & data

Does ZeroBased see my bank password?

No. You sign in to your bank through Plaid, and ZeroBased only receives read-only account data like balances and transactions.

Where can I read the full privacy policy?

It's at zerobased.org/privacy. Our Terms of Use are there too.

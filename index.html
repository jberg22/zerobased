The core idea

Spending against the calendar, not just the total.

$900 left means something different on the 5th than on the 25th. ZeroBased draws an even line from the first of the month to your budget and tracks your spending against it, so a big purchase early on shows up as a heads-up instead of a surprise at the end.

Security & privacy

Private by design.

Your financial data is personal. Here's how ZeroBased handles it.

Your bank login stays with your bank

Accounts connect through Plaid. ZeroBased never sees or stores your bank username or password.

Read-only access

ZeroBased can read balances and transactions. It can't move money or make payments.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Data is encrypted between your phone and our servers, and while it's stored.

Yours to keep or delete

We don't sell your data. Delete your account from the app anytime, and your data goes with it.

Questions

Which banks work with ZeroBased?

Thousands of US banks, credit unions and card issuers connect through Plaid. You can search for yours from the app's demo before you sign up.

Do I need to connect a bank to try it?

No. The demo runs on sample data, so you can see how everything works first.

Can ZeroBased move my money?

No. Access is read-only. ZeroBased can see balances and transactions, but it can't make transfers or payments.

How does the free trial work?

Your trial starts when you subscribe after creating an account. If you don't cancel before it ends, your subscription begins and is billed through your Apple ID.

Is ZeroBased on iPad or the web?

We're starting with iPhone. Leave your email above to hear when more arrives.

Who makes ZeroBased?

ZeroBased is made by ZeroBased LLC, an independent company. Reach us anytime at support@zerobased.org.
